Internship Reflection

OVERALL REFLECTION

Rotating across all four departments provided a real-world look into broadcast media, showing me how administrative setup, technical execution, news writing, and field reporting work together to run a functional station.

REFLECTION 01

Admin Department

Starting in Admin showed me how much backend work goes into keeping a station running smoothly. Sorting promo slips and clearing old blocktime data wasn't just routine tasks—it made me realize that good organization and archiving are what keep station operations organized and clients trusting us.

REFLECTION 02

Technical Department

Stepping into the technical booth was intense, especially during live broadcasts. Handling graphics, timing ad blocks, and editing packages on Filmora under live-air pressure taught me that technical operators are the station's real backbone—you have to keep your cool and think fast when unexpected glitches happen.

REFLECTION 03

Newsroom Department

Writing news in Hiligaynon and angling stories for social media was a huge eye-opener. From local council updates to breaking news, I learned how to craft headlines that catch attention while realizing that double-checking your work before hitting publish is non-negotiable—one bad mistake can ruin the station's credibility with the public.

REFLECTION 04

Reportorial Department

Being out in the field was where everything clicked. Riding along on mobile patrols, covering fire incidents, and jumping into unexpected live Q&As with anchors forced me out of my comfort zone. It taught me how to ask the right questions under pressure, respect source privacy, build a thick skin when turned away, and keep up with the fast pace of real-world reporting.
